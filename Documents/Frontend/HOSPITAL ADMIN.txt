# 🏥 ROLE 4 — HOSPITAL ADMIN

We will have **1 Hospital Admin** in our initial MediMind setup.

The Hospital Admin is responsible for managing the **entire hospital**, including its departments and Department Heads.

The hierarchy is:

```text id="0qg1ec"
👑 Chairman / Platform Owner
              ↓
       🏥 Hospital Admin
              ↓
      ┌───────┼────────┐
      ↓       ↓        ↓
     🦴      🩺       ❤️
 Orthopedics Diabetology Cardiology
      ↓       ↓        ↓
 Dept Head Dept Head Dept Head
      ↓       ↓        ↓
  3 Doctors 3 Doctors 3 Doctors
```

---

# 1. 🏥 Hospital Admin Account

The Hospital Admin **cannot register themselves**.

The account is created by the:

> 👑 **Chairman / Platform Owner**

Flow:

```text id="3j0y2x"
Chairman
   ↓
Creates Hospital Admin
   ↓
Assigns Hospital
   ↓
Hospital Admin Login
```

The Chairman enters:

* Admin name
* Email
* Phone
* Password
* Hospital
* Admin information

For our prototype:

> **1 Hospital → 1 Hospital Admin**

                         │

        │                │                │

So the Hospital Admin is the **middle management layer** between the Chairman and the three Department Heads.

---

## 🔑 One important architectural rule

Our hierarchy now has a very clean separation:

```text id="m3i8ar"
ORGANIZATIONAL ACCESS

Chairman
   ↓
Hospital Admin
   ↓
Department Head
   ↓
Doctor


MEDICAL DATA ACCESS

Family Account
   ↓
Specific Family Member
   ↓
Share with Doctor
   ↓
Doctor accesses authorized records
```

This separation should be reflected in our **JWT roles + authorization middleware + MongoDB data model + REST APIs**.
