# 🧑‍⚕️ ROLE 3 — DEPARTMENT HEAD

We will have exactly **3 Department Heads**:

| Department     | AI Area            | Department Head |
| -------------- | ------------------ | --------------- |
| 🦴 Orthopedics | Fracture Detection | 1               |
| 🩺 Diabetology | Diabetes Risk      | 1               |
| ❤️ Cardiology  | Heart Disease Risk | 1               |

A Department Head is responsible for **managing their department and its doctors**.

They do **not** have unrestricted access to patient medical records.

---

# 1. 🧑‍⚕️ Department Head Account Creation

Department Heads **cannot register themselves**.

The hierarchy is:

```text id="j5g4bq"
👑 Chairman
      ↓
🏥 Hospital Admin
      ↓
🧑‍⚕️ Department Head
```

The Hospital Admin creates the Department Head account.

The Hospital Admin provides:

* Name
* Email
* Phone
* Password
* Department
* Hospital
* Profile information

The Department Head then logs in normally.

---

# 2. 🔐 Login

For now:

```text id="2e9w8w"
Email
Password
   ↓
Login
   ↓
Department Dashboard
```

The backend identifies:

```text id="2w5r9e"
User
├── Role: DEPARTMENT_HEAD
├── Hospital ID
└── Department ID
```

The Department Head can only manage **their assigned department**.

# 5. ➕ Create Doctor

This is the Department Head's key administrative responsibility.

The Department Head creates the doctor account.

```text id="s8cl5h"
Create Doctor

Name
Email
Phone
Password

Experience
Specialization
Profile Photo

Department:
Orthopedics 🔒

Hospital:
MediMind Hospital 🔒

```

### Important

The Department Head **cannot change**:

* Hospital
* Department

because these are determined by the organization's hierarchy.

For example:

> Orthopedics Department Head cannot create a Cardiology doctor.

# 7. 🔄 Doctor Account Management

Department Head can:

* Create doctor
* View doctor
* Edit doctor information
* Activate doctor
* Deactivate doctor
* View doctor availability
* View doctor workload

But:

* ❌ Cannot change hospital
* ❌ Cannot move doctor to another department directly
* ❌ Cannot view doctor's private account password
* ❌ Cannot access unrelated departments

---

# 8. 🕐 Schedules

The Department Head manages the **department schedule**.

Example:

```text id="o2v6te"
Orthopedics Schedule

Monday

Dr. Rahul
09:00 – 13:00

Dr. Kumar
14:00 – 18:00

Dr. Anil
09:00 – 13:00
```

They can:

* View doctor availability
* Coordinate schedules
* Detect overlapping schedules
* Manage department-level schedule
* View blocked dates

The doctor can still manage their own availability within the allowed structure.

# 14. 👤 Patient Information

This needs careful permission handling.

Department Head **should not have access to all medical records**.

They can see operational statistics such as:

* Number of patients
* Number of appointments
* Department workload

But:

```text id="c7s7ca"
Patient Medical Record
       ↓
Family + Authorized Doctor
```

not:

```text id="6f1y0b"
Patient Medical Record
       ↓
Department Head automatically ❌
```

This maintains the access-control model we've already established.

---

# 15. 📚 MediMind Knowledge

Department Heads are also doctors/professionals, so they can participate in the knowledge system.

They can:

* Read doctor articles
* Publish knowledge articles
* Edit their own articles
* View department-related knowledge
* Share useful medical observations

Example:

```text id="p2u6bq"
MediMind Knowledge

"Early Signs of Bone Fracture"
Dr. Rahul

"Common Risk Indicators"
Dr. Priya

"Post-Fracture Follow-up Practices"
Dr. Kumar
```

We can optionally give Department Heads the ability to **review articles from their department before publication** if we want a moderation workflow.

For the first version, I'd keep it simple:

> Doctors can publish their own articles; Department Heads can also publish/read.

---


Department Head receives:

* New doctor account created
* Doctor account status changes
* Schedule conflicts
* Appointment statistics
* Department notifications
* Hospital Admin announcements
* System notifications

# 🎯 Example: Orthopedics Department

Our actual project data would look like:

```text id="8c6i9r"
🏥 MediMind Hospital
        │
        └── 🦴 Orthopedics
                │
                ├── 🧑‍⚕️ Dept Head
                │
                ├── 👨‍⚕️ Dr. Rahul
                ├── 👨‍⚕️ Dr. Kumar
                └── 👨‍⚕️ Dr. Anil
                         │
                         ↓
                  Fracture Detection
                         │
                         ↓
                    CNN / AI
```

The same structure exists for:

```text
🩺 Diabetology
├── Dept Head
├── Doctor 1
├── Doctor 2
└── Doctor 3
       ↓
Diabetes Risk Prediction
```

and

```text
❤️ Cardiology
├── Dept Head
├── Doctor 1
├── Doctor 2
└── Doctor 3
       ↓
Heart Disease Risk Prediction
```

So the **Department Head is primarily an organizational/management role**, while the **Doctor is the clinical role**.
