# 👑 ROLE 5 — CHAIRMAN / PLATFORM OWNER

The **Chairman / Platform Owner** is the highest-level administrative role in MediMind.

For our initial prototype:

> **1 Chairman**

The Chairman manages the **entire MediMind platform/network**, while the Hospital Admin manages only their hospital.

---

# 1. 👑 Chairman Account

The Chairman account is the **root platform account**.

For our academic prototype, we can create this account directly in the database during initial setup.

```text id="w7m2kf"
👑 Chairman
      │
      └── 🏥 Hospital Admin
              │
              ├── 🦴 Orthopedics Head
              ├── 🩺 Diabetology Head
              └── ❤️ Cardiology Head
```

The Chairman **does not register through the normal public registration page**.

# 18. 📝 Audit Logs

This is an important platform security feature.

Chairman can view important system actions.

Example:

```text id="f8o4nq"
Audit Logs

15 Sep 2026 10:30
Hospital Admin created Department Head

15 Sep 2026 11:00
Department Head created Doctor

15 Sep 2026 11:25
Family granted doctor access

15 Sep 2026 12:10
Doctor completed consultation
```

The audit log should record things like:

* Who performed the action
* What action occurred
* Which entity was affected
* Timestamp
* Relevant service

This is especially useful for demonstrating **security + microservices + accountability**.

## 🔑 And the two access systems remain separate

### Organizational hierarchy

**Chairman → Hospital Admin → Department Head → Doctor**

### Medical access hierarchy

**Family Account → Family Member → Share → Doctor**

That is now a very clean foundation for the project.
