# 👨‍⚕️ ROLE 2 — DOCTOR

## 1. Basic Concept

A **Doctor** is a medical professional working within one hospital and one department.

Doctors are **not allowed to self-register**.

Their account is created by the **Department Head** of their department.

```text
🏥 Hospital
     │
     ├── 🦴 Orthopedics
     │      └── 👨‍⚕️ Doctors
     │
     ├── 🩺 Diabetology
     │      └── 👨‍⚕️ Doctors
     │
     └── ❤️ Cardiology
            └── 👨‍⚕️ Doctors
```

Each doctor:

* Works in exactly **one hospital**
* Belongs to exactly **one department**
* Has exactly **one specialization**
* Has their own professional profile
* Can manage their own availability
* Can consult authorized patients
* Can create consultations
* Can issue prescriptions
* Can create medical knowledge articles
* Can view AI-assisted predictions for authorized patients

---

# 2. Doctor Account Creation

Doctors cannot create their own accounts.

The account creation hierarchy is:

```text
Chairman
   ↓
Hospital Admin
   ↓
Department Head
   ↓
Doctor Account
```

### Doctor creation flow

```text
Department Head
      ↓
Create Doctor
      ↓
Enter Doctor Information
      ↓
Create Account
      ↓
Doctor receives/login credentials
      ↓
Doctor Login
```

The Department Head can only create doctors belonging to **their own department**.

---

# 3. Doctor Registration

There is **no public Doctor Registration page**.

If a doctor tries to register independently:

```text
Doctor Registration
        ↓
      ❌ Not Allowed
```

Instead:

```text
Department Head
        ↓
Create Doctor Account
        ↓
Doctor Login
```

For the current version, doctors use:

> **Normal email/password login**

No OTP or Google authentication is required initially.

# 5. Doctor Organizational Restrictions

A doctor is restricted to one organizational location.

```text
Doctor
  │
  ├── Hospital → ONE
  │
  ├── Department → ONE
  │
  └── Specialization → ONE
```

Example:

```text
Dr. Rahul
    │
    ├── Hospital: MediMind Hospital
    ├── Department: Orthopedics
    └── Specialization: Orthopedic Specialist
```

The doctor cannot simultaneously belong to:

```text
Orthopedics + Cardiology ❌
```

or:

```text
Hospital A + Hospital B ❌
```

# 9. 👥 Patients Page

The Patients page shows **only patients who have authorized this doctor**.

This is extremely important.

A doctor does **not** automatically see all patients belonging to the hospital.

Example:

```text
Family Account
      │
      ├── Father
      ├── Mother
      └── Son
```

If the family authorizes:

```text
Mother → Dr. Rahul
```

then:

```text
Dr. Rahul
    ↓
Patients
    ↓
Mother
```

The doctor cannot automatically see:

```text
Father ❌
Son ❌
```

unless those patients are separately authorized.

# 17. 🔒 Consultation Immutability

Once a doctor submits/finalizes a consultation:

> **The original consultation cannot be edited or deleted.**

This protects medical-history integrity.

```text
Doctor creates consultation
          ↓
       Draft
          ↓
       Submit
          ↓
       FINAL
          ↓
     IMMUTABLE
```

If the doctor discovers an error:

```text
Original Consultation
        ↓
      Final
        ↓
   Immutable
        ↓
Amendment / Correction
        ↓
New linked record
```

Therefore, the system maintains an auditable medical history instead of silently changing historical records.

# 21. 📜 Doctor Access History

Unlike the family side, we decided that the **doctor should be able to see their own patient authorization/access history**.

Example:

```text
Access History

Patient: Priya Sharma

15 Sep 2026
Access Granted

16 Sep 2026
Access Active

20 Sep 2026
Access Revoked
```

The purpose is to allow the doctor to understand:

* Which patients authorized them
* When access was granted
* Current access status
* When access was revoked

This is an authorization history, not unrestricted access to other patients.

# 22. 🚫 Unauthorized Patient Protection

If a doctor attempts to open an unauthorized patient's records:

```text
Doctor
  ↓
Patient Request
  ↓
Authorization Check
  ↓
Not Authorized
  ↓
❌ Access Denied
```

Example:

```text
Patient: Father
Doctor: Dr. Rahul

Authorization:
Not Granted

❌ You do not have access to this patient's medical records.
```

This must be enforced by the backend, not only hidden in the React UI.

```text

   ↓
```

```text
   ↓
```

Incorrect:

```text

   ↓

   ↓
```

This is a core privacy/security rule in MediMind.

# ⭐ 39. Complete Role 2 Concept

The Doctor role can be summarized as:

```text
                         👨‍⚕️ DOCTOR
                              │
             ┌────────────────┼────────────────┐
             ↓                ↓                ↓
        👥 Patients      📅 Appointments    📚 Knowledge
             │                │                │
             ↓                ↓                ↓
      Authorized Only     Booked Slots      Articles
             │                │                │
             ↓                ↓                ↓
      🗂️ Medical Records  📋 Consultation   Review
             │                │                │
             ↓                ↓                ↓
       🤖 AI Results      💊 Prescription    Publish
             │
             ↓
       AI Explanation
             │
             ↓
       Clinical Review
```

### Core principles

> **A doctor can access only patients who have explicitly authorized that doctor.**

> **Authorization is patient-specific, not family-wide.**

> **Once authorized, the doctor receives access to the complete record set of that patient.**

> **Finalized consultations and prescriptions are immutable; corrections use amendments or new records.**

> **AI provides decision support and explainability, while the doctor remains responsible for clinical assessment.**

> **Doctors can create medical knowledge, but publication requires Department Head review.**

> **Published knowledge may be publicly accessible, while private patient information remains protected.**

> **Doctors cannot self-register, create other doctors, manage departments, or automatically access hospital-wide patient records.**
