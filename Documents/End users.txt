👥 MediMind User Roles
Role	Level	Main Purpose
👨‍👩‍👧 Family Account	End user	Manage family members, health records, appointments, and authorized record sharing
👨‍⚕️ Doctor	Medical user	Treat patients and review authorized medical information
🧑‍⚕️ Department Head	Medical management	Manage doctors and oversee a department
🏥 Hospital Admin	Hospital management	Manage hospital-level operations
👑 Chairman / Platform Owner	Highest level	Manage and monitor the entire MediMind network
1. 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Account

The primary healthcare end user.

One family creates one account, with individual profiles for each family member.

Family Account
│
├── Father
├── Mother
├── Son
└── Daughter

Each family member has a separate medical profile and medical history.

Can:
Create and manage family profiles
Add/remove family members
Upload medical reports
Upload scans and medical images
Upload prescriptions
Upload previous test results
Maintain individual medical histories
Book doctor appointments
View prescriptions and consultation records
Use AI prediction and General Health Assessment features
View AI prediction history
Share a specific member's records with an authorized doctor
Grant/revoke doctor access
Manage family appointments
🔐 Important

The family account doesn't mean everyone can automatically see every member's medical information.

For example:

Family Account
      │
      ├── Father → 🔒 Medical Records
      ├── Mother → 🔒 Medical Records
      └── Child  → 🔒 Medical Records

Doctor access is granted separately for each selected family member. An authorized doctor receives complete medical-record access for that member.

2. 👨‍⚕️ Doctor

The main healthcare professional.

Can:
Create/manage doctor profile
Manage availability
View patients who have authorized access
Review previous reports and tests
View medical history
View uploaded scans/images
View AI predictions
Review AI confidence/explanation
Add consultation notes
Add clinical assessment
Add prescriptions
Manage appointments
Follow patient history
🔐 Important

A doctor cannot simply search for and open every patient's records.

Patient/Family
      ↓
Grants Access
      ↓
Doctor
      ↓
Authorized Records
3. 🧑‍⚕️ Department Head

A Department Head manages doctors belonging to a particular department.

For example:

Hospital
│
├── Cardiology
│     ├── Department Head
│     ├── Doctor
│     ├── Doctor
│     └── Doctor
│
├── Neurology
│     ├── Department Head
│     ├── Doctor
│     └── Doctor
│
└── Radiology
      ├── Department Head
      ├── Doctor
      └── Doctor
Can:
Manage doctors within their department
Assign doctors to schedules
Manage department schedules
View department appointments
Monitor department workload
View aggregated department statistics
View department performance
Manage department-level operations
🔐 Medical privacy

The Department Head doesn't automatically receive unrestricted access to every patient's complete medical records.

Patient authorization and appropriate medical access rules still apply.

4. 🏥 Hospital Admin

Manages the operations of a particular hospital.

Can:
Manage hospital information
Create/manage departments
Manage Department Heads
Manage doctors and staff
Manage hospital schedules
Manage hospital appointments
Manage staff accounts
View hospital-level analytics
View department-level statistics
Handle hospital configuration
Manage hospital operational settings
Organizational hierarchy
Hospital Admin
       ↓
Department Head
       ↓
     Doctor

This is an organizational hierarchy, not automatically a medical-record-access hierarchy.

5. 👑 Chairman / Platform Owner

The highest-level MediMind role.

I recommend calling it:

Chairman / Platform Owner

This represents the person/organization responsible for the entire MediMind network.

                  👑 Chairman
                Platform Owner

          ↓            ↓            ↓
      Departments  Departments  Departments
          ↓            ↓            ↓
        Doctors      Doctors      Doctors
Can:
Register/approve hospitals
Manage Hospital Admins
Monitor all hospitals
View platform-wide analytics
View hospital performance
View department performance
Monitor appointment statistics
View overall AI prediction statistics
Monitor platform usage
View system reports
Manage platform-level policies
View audit logs
Manage the MediMind network

🔐 Final Permission Structure

There are two separate structures.

🏢 Organizational hierarchy
                 👑 Chairman
                     │
                     ↓
              🏥 Hospital Admin
                     │
                     ↓
             🧑‍⚕️ Department Head
                     │
                     ↓
                👨‍⚕️ Doctor
🩺 Medical-record access
        👨‍👩‍👧 Family Account
                 │
                 ↓
        Select Family Member
                 │
                 ↓
        Grant Doctor Access
                 │
                 ↓
              👨‍⚕️ Doctor
                 │
                 ↓
       Authorized Medical Records

The organizational hierarchy does NOT automatically grant medical-record access.

For example, a Hospital Admin could see:

"Hospital has 2,500 registered patients."

But that doesn't mean they can automatically open every patient's X-ray, prescription, or medical history.
