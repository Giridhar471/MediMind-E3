# 2. 🗂️ Medical Records

### Family

* Upload → ✅
* View → ✅
* Edit own uploaded record → ✅
* Delete own uploaded record → ✅
* View any family member's records → ✅

### Doctor

* View records → **✅ after patient chooses that doctor**
* Edit family-uploaded records → ❌
* Delete family-uploaded records → ❌
* Add clinical records → ✅

### Administration

* Department Head → ❌ individual records
* Hospital Admin → ❌ individual records
* Chairman → ❌ individual records

So the core rule is:

> **Administrative authority does not equal medical-record authority.**

# 3. 🔐 Doctor Access — Updated Rule

Your new decision changes the workflow slightly.

You want:

> **When a family member chooses a doctor, that doctor receives access to that member's complete medical history.**

So we don't need a complicated "select individual record" sharing system.

### Flow

```text id="r5w8e2"
Family
   ↓
Select Family Member
   ↓
Select Doctor
   ↓
"Choose Doctor"
   ↓
Access Granted
   ↓
Doctor receives ALL records
   ↓
Doctor can use Unified Medical Record
```

### Shared access contains

```text id="f7k5z1"
Everything
├── Medical Reports
├── Test Results
├── X-Rays / Scans
├── Prescriptions
├── Previous Consultations
├── AI Predictions
├── Medical History
└── Other Medical Records
```

This is much simpler and better for your **Unified Medical Record** concept.

# 11. 🩺 Consultation

Here's the decision I recommend:

### Once a doctor submits a consultation:

> **The original consultation cannot be edited or deleted.**

Instead, if the doctor makes a mistake:

```text id="0g8w0m"
Original Consultation
       ↓
Locked / Immutable
       ↓
Doctor adds Amendment
       ↓
New timestamp + reason
```

This is better for a medical-history system because we maintain a trustworthy history.

# 16. 👤 Account Deactivation & Deletion

You said **both options**.

So we'll support:

### Temporary

**Deactivate**

```text
Account
 ↓
Deactivated
 ↓
Cannot login
 ↓
Data retained
```

### Permanent

**Delete**

```text
Account
 ↓
Deletion request
 ↓
Data deletion process
```

For doctors/admins, we'll generally prefer **deactivation** because their historical clinical/administrative records may need to remain intact.

# 🔥 Final Core Permission Rules

These are now the rules I would consider **locked**:

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Family

> One login → multiple member profiles → all members can access all family information.

### 👤 Family Creator

> Creates/removes members and can deactivate/delete the entire family account.

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor

> Cannot register; Department Head creates the account.

### 🏥 Hospital

> One doctor → one hospital → one department → one specialization.

### 🔐 Medical Access

> Family chooses a doctor → that doctor gets access to **all records of that selected family member**.

### 🚫 Admin Clinical Access

> Department Heads, Hospital Admins and Chairman do **not** automatically receive patient medical records.

### 🩺 Clinical Records

> Final consultations and prescriptions are **immutable**; corrections use amendments/new records.

### 📚 Knowledge

> Doctor writes → Department Head approves → published knowledge becomes publicly readable and useful to medical staff.

### 🤖 AI

> Three AI modules only:
>
> **Fracture Detection + Diabetes Risk + Heart Disease Risk**

### 📝 Audit

> Hospital Admin sees hospital-wide audit activity; Chairman sees platform-wide audit activity.
